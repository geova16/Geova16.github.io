FarmBot is an automated mini-farm with an irrigation system and plant recognition, developed at the GPAR laboratory of the Federal University of Ceará.

## What I did

- Trained the image recognition algorithm that identifies the plants
- Maintained the electronic components of the system

## Publication

The project led to a paper on the development and experimental analysis of a robotic system for automated vegetable planting, using precision agriculture and computer vision, published in the *Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Systems* (vol. 7, pp. 35–60, 2025). [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.33969/AIS.2025070103).
