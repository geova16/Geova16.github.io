A project carried out at PET Elétrica (Programa de Educação Tutorial, an excellence programme) of the Federal University of Ceará, combining engineering and sustainability: turning recycled PET bottles into filament for 3D printers.

The work was presented at the Encontros Universitários UFC in 2024.
